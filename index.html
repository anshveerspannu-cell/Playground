Quick tips

Arcade

Arcade Match

Move with Arrow keys / WASD (or drag on touch). Run into the ball to dribble it and it sticks to you until you shoot (Space) or get tackled. Hold Up or Down while shooting to aim at the corners. Press Z to pass to a teammate. Press Space near an opponent who has the ball to tackle, but rough tackles can be fouls: 🟨 yellow cards for either side, and in 5v5 and 11v11 a second yellow or a 🟥 red sends the player off for the match. Score more than the CPU in 60 seconds. Win = +60 to +120 🪙 depending on match size, draw = +15.

About

ABOUT PLAYGROUND

Created by Abir and Anshveer.

Playground.com is a football arcade world: quick matches, squad upgrades, fan polls, a quiz and a library of articles to help you play better.

Everything runs in your browser. Coins and upgrades are saved on your device only, so there is no signup and nothing to install.

Controls: Arrow keys or WASD to move, Space to shoot or tackle. On touch, drag to move and tap Kick.
